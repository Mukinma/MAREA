## Direction

**Corrientes locales** is the visual language for MAREA. It uses the existing blue/aqua palette as a confident social identity, then gives it a warmer paper ground and more expressive composition so the product feels lived-in instead of administrative.

## Surface rules

- Mobile is the reference breakpoint: 16 px page gutters, 48 px minimum targets, and a 76 px bottom navigation with the create action as the visual anchor.
- Community screens use a compact MAREA masthead, a strong page title, a short purpose statement, and content that starts within one thumb scroll.
- Posts are airy, border-led surfaces with author context first, content second, and save/report actions last.
- Mint and pastel category colors are contextual accents only. Navy and action blue own hierarchy and interaction.
- Desktop uses the same reading order in a constrained 960 px column beside the extended navigation rail.

## Accessibility

Text remains high-contrast on paper and white surfaces. Actions use labels/tooltips and icons together where space allows; empty, loading and error states stay explicit.
